# 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science student and the founder of **IgnisFlow**, a digital presence and AI automation agency for small businesses.

I design, deploy, and maintain **self-hosted production infrastructure** — Docker, reverse proxies, observability, and automation pipelines running on a VPS I administer end to end.

My focus is **Cloud and DevOps**. I'm currently working through the AWS certification path (CLF-C02 → SAA-C03 → DevOps Pro) and turning the systems I build for real clients into the foundation of that career.
